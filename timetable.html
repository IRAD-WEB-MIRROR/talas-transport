Timetables: summer and winter

Today: the summer timetable.

The Bus Park runs two timetables. The winter timetable starts on the first day of snow or hard frost in the city, and on 1 December at the latest (Mayor's order 418-r of 26 October 2026). The summer timetable returns in spring, with the first warm days and by the end of March at the latest. This site follows the official season of the Office of the Governor and changes by itself.

What changes in winter

  • Buses start 15 minutes later and the last trips leave about 40 minutes earlier.
  • Intervals are 3 minutes longer.
  • Marshrutkas: the first and last trips of each route are set in its licence; most carriers start 15-30 minutes later and stop 30-60 minutes earlier, and run fewer trips after 19:00.
  • Snow days: routes to Alatau Heights turn short when the road above the Conservatory is not cleared: 10, 16, 18, 19, 35, 43, 64, 66, 78, 81, 109, 113, 129, 131, 157, 169, 189, 192.
  • Buses are heated. Please close the doors behind you on the old LiAZ buses.
