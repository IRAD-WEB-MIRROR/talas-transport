News

20.10.2026

Winter preparation finished

All 71 buses have passed the winter inspection. The workshop has fitted winter tyres to the whole fleet; the two snow-ploughs of the Bus Park are on duty for the depot yard and the turning circles from 15 November.

15.09.2026

Drivers wanted

The Bus Park takes on bus drivers with category D, and mechanics. Salary from 2,600 som, free travel, a hot meal at the canteen. Apply to the personnel department, room 9.

03.07.2026

Air-conditioning in the Yutong buses

Please keep the windows shut in the buses with air-conditioning (routes 4 and 11). The drivers are asked to switch it on above 28 degrees.
