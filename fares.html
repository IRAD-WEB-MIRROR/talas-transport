Travel cards are sold at the Bus Park cash desk (Drivers' Street 7, Mon-Fri 8:00-17:00), at the Central Bus Station kiosk and at the Central Square kiosk (by the Opera Theatre). The social travel card is issued by the City Hall's Social department, room 210.

Concessions are given on municipal buses only. On marshrutkas everyone pays 2 som; some drivers carry pensioners free, but they do not have to.

Rules for passengers

  1. Enter by the front door, leave by the middle and back doors.
  2. Give up your seat to the elderly, passengers with children and people with disabilities.
  3. Smoking, sunflower seeds and alcohol are not allowed in the bus.
  4. Animals in a basket or on a lead with a muzzle; small animals free, dogs 2 som.
  5. Ticket checks: the fine for travel without a ticket is 10 som.
