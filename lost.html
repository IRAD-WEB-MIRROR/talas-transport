Lost property

Lost property office: Bus Park, Drivers' Street 7, room 4 (entrance from the yard).
Mon-Fri 9:00-17:00, lunch 13:00-14:00. Tel. 41-15-22.

Things left in municipal buses are handed in by the drivers at the end of the shift. Marshrutka carriers bring what they find once a week. We keep things for three months; after that they go to charity (clothes to the Church of St. Yuhanna, books to the city libraries). Food is not kept.

Passports, ID cards and other documents go to the Gendarmerie the next day and are not listed here. Bring your ID to collect a thing and describe it.
